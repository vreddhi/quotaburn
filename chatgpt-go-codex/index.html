ChatGPT Go and Codex: what $8 gets you

Codex is included on every ChatGPT plan, but the allowance and features differ a lot.

Data verified 2026-10-19 from official pricing pages.

What OpenAI says

  • Price: ChatGPT Go is $8/month in the US; pricing is localized in some markets. OpenAI announced worldwide availability on Jan 16, 2026.
  • Codex access: Codex is included across ChatGPT plans, including Free and Go. Usage limits vary by plan, and OpenAI's pricing page lists Codex as “Limited” on Free and Go.
  • Codex Cloud: available to eligible Plus, all Pro tiers, Business, Enterprise and Edu accounts. It is not included with Free or Go.
  • Shared allowance: local messages and cloud chats share your plan's usage allowance, and weekly limits may also apply.

OpenAI doesn't publish a message count for Go's Codex allowance, so we don't give one.

Go vs Plus

Go is $8/month and Plus is $20/month. For Plus, OpenAI's estimate is: Plus: rolling 5-hour window plus possible weekly limits. OpenAI's estimate for Plus is 15-160 local GPT-6.1 Sol messages, 15-150 GPT-6 Sol, 5-45 GPT-6 Astra, or 350-3,000 GPT-6 Luna messages per 5 hours.

Choose Go to try Codex locally on a budget. Choose Plus if you need Codex Cloud tasks or a bigger allowance. Compare with other tools at plans at $20 or less and Codex pricing.

How much is ChatGPT Go?

$8 per month in the US, per OpenAI. Pricing is localized in some markets.

Does ChatGPT Go include Codex?

Yes, with limited access. Codex Cloud is not included on Go; it needs Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise or Edu.

How many Codex messages do I get on Go?

OpenAI doesn't publish a number. Usage depends on the model, task size, context and whether work runs locally or in the cloud.
