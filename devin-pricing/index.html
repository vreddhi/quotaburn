Usage limits

Self-serve plans include a quota that refreshes daily and weekly; overage is billed as dollar-denominated on-demand credits at API pricing. ACUs remain for Enterprise only. Quota sizes are not published in tokens.

ACUs vs quota

ACUs now apply to Enterprise only. Self-serve plans get a quota that refreshes daily and weekly. Overage is billed as on-demand credits in dollars at API pricing. Devin doesn't publish quota sizes in tokens.
